# Design System Architecture

This document preserves the future design system specifications for the **AIML Department Student Academic Management System** at **DVR & Dr. HS MIC College of Technology**.

---

## 1. HOD Theme (Administrative Interface)

Designed for authority, high readability, analytical precision, and administrative clarity.

- **Sage Fog (60%)**: Primary base background and workspace canvas tone.
- **Claret Wine (30%)**: Structural elements, sidebars, headers, cards, and primary content groupings.
- **Tangerine Flash (10%)**: Accent, focal points, primary action triggers, and active status indicators.
  - **Hex Code:** `#FF6B1A`

### Specialized Interaction:
- **Spotlight Interaction**: Navigation links and primary metric panels use radial spotlight cursor-following gradients for subtle ambient feedback.

---

## 2. Student Theme (Student Interface)

Designed for warmth, engagement, accessible readability, and intuitive discovery.

- **Almond Milk (60%)**: Clean, warm, comfortable background canvas.
- **Dusty Teal (30%)**: Secondary cards, content sections, course modules, and progress indicators.
- **Cherry Soda (10%)**: Dynamic accent, key CTAs, notifications, and alerts.
  - **Hex Code:** `#E8213A`

### Specialized Interaction:
- **Spotlight and Bounce Interactions**: Interactive navigation items feature subtle spotlight luminescence combined with spring-physics micro-bounce interactions on hover and tap.

---

## 3. Shared Architectural Styling Rules

- **Strict Palette Separation**: Do NOT mix the HOD and Student colour palettes under any circumstances.
- **Glassmorphism**: Subtle frosted glass surfaces (`backdrop-blur-md`, subtle border highlights) on floating panels and modals.
- **Claymorphism**: Soft double-inner shadows with rounded corners on metric cards and buttons to provide depth.
- **Premium Floating Navigation**: Detached, floating navigation elements with soft drop shadows and responsive mobile behavior.
- **Responsive Mobile Layouts**: First-class mobile accessibility across phones, tablets, and desktop workstations.

---

*Note: As per Phase 0 guidelines, complete dashboard views and final styling will be implemented in subsequent development phases.*
